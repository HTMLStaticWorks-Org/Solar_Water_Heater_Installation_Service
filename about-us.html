Our Story

Over a Decade of Clean Solar Thermal Innovation

Founded in 2012, SunTherm Solar began with a single mission: helping families eliminate expensive electric geyser bills by harnessing abundant rooftop sunlight. Early solar heaters suffered from hard-water scaling, fragile tubes, and heat loss overnight.

We re-engineered the entire system. Today, SunTherm provides BIS-certified triple-layer ETC and ultrasonic copper FPC solar water heaters featuring 55mm PUF insulation and anti-scale glass-lined inner tanks—delivering 48-hour hot water retention and 80% electricity bill savings.
