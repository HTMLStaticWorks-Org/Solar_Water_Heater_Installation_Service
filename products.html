Capacity & Product FAQs

How do I choose between 150 LPD and 200 LPD?

150 LPD is ideal for 3–4 people with 2 bathrooms. Choose 200 LPD for 4–5 people, 3 bathrooms, or if you have frequent guests.

What is the difference between ETC and FPC?

ETC uses borosilicate vacuum glass tubes (cost-effective and great in cold climates), while FPC uses metallic copper fins under toughened glass (ideal for pressurized systems and longevity).

How much roof space does a 200 LPD system need?

A 200 LPD solar water heater requires approximately 7 ft x 7 ft (around 50 sq. ft.) of shadow-free south-facing rooftop space.
