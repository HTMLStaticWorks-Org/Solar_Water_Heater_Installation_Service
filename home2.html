Frequently Asked Questions

How much electricity bill will a 200 LPD solar water heater save?

A 200 LPD solar water heater replaces 2 to 3 electric geysers, saving approximately 2,400 to 2,800 kWh of electricity annually—which translates to ₹18,000 to ₹22,000 saved every year.

Will I get hot water during monsoon or cloudy winter days?

Yes! High-density 55mm PUF insulation keeps stored water hot for up to 48 hours. Additionally, all our systems include an optional thermostatic electric backup coil for continuous overcast days.

Which system is best if our home uses borewell hard water?

For hard water (TDS above 400 ppm), we recommend our Ceramic/Glass-Lined Tank ETC models or Closed-Loop Heat Exchanger FPC systems, which prevent mineral scaling and corrosion.
